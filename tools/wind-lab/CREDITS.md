# Wind Lab — credits and licences

The engine code (presets.js, engine.js, app.js) is part of ScoreDAW.

## Recordings (samples/ and packs/)
| File | Instrument | Author | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tenorsax | Tenor saxophone — VCSL (Versilian Community Sample Library): non-vibrato sustain, vibrato and staccato | Versilian Studios (Samuel Gossner and contributors) | **CC0 1.0** (public domain) | https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL |
| trumpet | Trumpet in B♭ — VSCO-2 Community Edition (played by Simon Hutchinson): sustain, vibrato, staccato, harmon mute, straight mute | Versilian Studios | **CC0 1.0** (public domain) | https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE |

Both libraries are CC0: free for any use, also commercial, with no attribution required. The credit
is kept here anyway, as thanks.

The recordings were converted by `convert_winds.py`: tuned to A = 440 Hz, leading silence trimmed,
a seamless crossfade loop added to the sustained notes, stereo OGG Vorbis at 32 kHz.

## Demo music
All demo phrases (ballad, bebop, funk, latin, long tones, dynamics, range) are original.
